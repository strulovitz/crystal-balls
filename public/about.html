How Crystal Balls works

This page is a placeholder. The full marketing page explaining the project and the method is written later (Bible Part 6, section 6.3 notes this page is "written later").

In short

Crystal Balls reads this week's headlines for 32 countries from 12 news sources and asks a local AI, running on a home computer, to write in plain words what happened and what it expects next week. That expectation is the plain forecast. Then the AI is shown how things moved since last week, and how last week's expectation met reality, and writes a second forecast by reasoning from the present, along the motion, and corrected for the way it was wrong before. That is the Crystal forecast.

A week later an AI judge reads what the news actually reported, writes a full comparison of the two forecasts in plain words, and ends with one verdict: Crystal, plain, or even. Every forecast, every essay and every verdict is in the public archive.

Everything is free, open source, and runs on a home computer. The code and all the data are on GitHub.
