A major split between UBS and the Swiss government.
An immediate market panic over UBS capital rules.
But I do expect:

intense lobbying,
parliamentary debate,
a vote or key committee decision,
and a compromise that keeps UBS in Switzerland but irritates some banking regulators or left-wing politicians.
