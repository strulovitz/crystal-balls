My prediction: within the next week, North Korea will announce at least one more missile-related or air-defense-related military activity. It could be:

a short-range ballistic missile launch,
an intermediate-range missile test,
cruise missile drills,
drone swarm exercises,
anti-aircraft missile drills,

The DMZ mine blast and Seoul’s demand for an apology have given Pyongyang a ready-made propaganda target. North Korea has already called the South Korean investigation a “farce” and dismissed the apology demand as pathetic or insulting.

Expect another official statement in the next few days saying something like:

Seoul is being provocative,
the mine blast was a premeditated incident,
demanding an apology is an insult to national dignity,

more train movements or logistical preparation,
additional units near the Russian border,
state media possibly hinting at “internationalist duty” or “solidarity,”
continued quiet coordination with Russia on equipment, ammunition, or troop deployment.

The drop in defections, the POW transfer issue, and the DMZ incident all suggest Pyongyang is worried about leaks, desertion, and information flow. Expect:

tighter control along the southern border,
more checkpoints or surveillance in border regions,
stricter internal propaganda,
possible increased punishment signals for desertion attempts,
more attention to military personnel assigned to Russia.
This is not necessarily a mass purge, but a “tighten the screws” phase.
